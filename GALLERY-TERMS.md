## 1. What the Gallery is

The App Gallery is a catalogue of small, self-contained apps ("gallery apps") that run inside MyLLM on your device, in a sandboxed web view. Some are made by us; some are contributed by the community (shown on the **Community** shelf and credited to their authors). Gallery apps are downloaded to your device when you tap **Get** and run entirely locally.

These terms cover use of the Gallery and submission of apps to it. Your use of the MyLLM app itself is covered by the MyLLM [Privacy Policy and Terms](https://www.opticell-limited.com/myllm) and the standard Apple licence agreement.

## 2. Using gallery apps

- **Everything runs on your device.** Gallery apps have no server of ours behind them. Opticell receives no data from your use of a gallery app — no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry.

- **Some apps talk to third-party services.** Apps with internet access may fetch data from external services (for example weather, news feeds or radio directories). Those services have their own terms and privacy policies, and we don't control them.

- **Don't rely on gallery apps for critical decisions.** Apps that produce measurements, health-adjacent information or AI-generated content are for general information only.

## 3. Content standards

Every app in the Gallery — ours or the community's — must be:

- **Family-friendly.** No pornographic, hateful, harassing or gratuitously violent content.
- **Honest.** No deceptive behaviour, hidden functionality, dark patterns or impersonation.
- **Safe.** No malware, no cryptocurrency mining, no attempts to escape the sandbox, no undisclosed data collection, no fingerprinting or tracking of the user.
