Feedback & Ideas

Tell us what to build next

Tell us what to build next, what's broken, or what you love. Write it here and send it whichever way suits you — nothing is uploaded on its own.

Send to MyLLM@opticell-limited.com, or open an issue at github.com/TeamDzX/myllm-assets. Every message is read by a human — you may not get a reply to each one, but it all feeds the roadmap.

Nothing leaves your phone by itself. This app has no internet access at all — it only builds the text above. It travels only when you tap send, and only to where you choose. Your draft is saved on-device so you don't lose it.
