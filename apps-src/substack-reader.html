Substack Reader

Follow publications, read free posts, join in on Substack
Paste a publication's web address, or just its name before ".substack.com". Publications on their own domain work too.

An unofficial reader. It talks directly to each publication's public site through MyLLM's network bridge, with no account and nothing stored anywhere but this device. Free posts open in full; paid posts show their preview. Liking, commenting and subscribing happen on Substack itself, where you are signed in.
