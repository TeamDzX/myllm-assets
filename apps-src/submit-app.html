🚀 Submit Your App

Built something good in MyLLM? Put it in this gallery, on iOS and Android, credited to you. This wizard checks the house rules and packages your submission — a human reviews every app before it goes live.

How to get your app's code: springboard → long-press your app → View Code → select all → copy. Then paste it below.

Send the package to MyLLM@opticell-limited.com or paste it into a GitHub issue at github.com/TeamDzX/myllm-assets. You'll hear back on the issue or by reply — most reviews take a few days. Submissions are covered by the Gallery Terms (github.com/TeamDzX/myllm-assets → GALLERY-TERMS.md) — in short: the code is yours to publish, you keep ownership, and you licence us to host and distribute it.
