Morse code

Tap a letter to hear it. A dash is three dots long; leave one dot between parts of a letter, three between letters and seven between words.

Good to know

  • SOS is • • • — — — • • • sent as one signal with no gaps between the letters, then a pause, then again.
  • Three of anything (three flashes, three whistle blasts, three fires in a row) is widely understood as a call for help.
  • Light carries much further at night. Aim at where people are likely to be and keep repeating.
  • Screen light uses less battery than the flashlight. Red keeps your night vision.
  • In a real emergency, call your local emergency number first if you can.
