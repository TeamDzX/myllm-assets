🎮 Retro Player

Bring your own game. Load a ROM you own and play it right here — NES, SNES, Game Boy / Color, GBA, and Sega Genesis.
Supports .nes · .sfc/.smc · .gb/.gbc · .gba · .md/.smd/.gen · .sms/.gg — or a .zip containing one.
No games are included — this is just the player. The first time you load a system it downloads that console's core, so it needs an internet connection.
