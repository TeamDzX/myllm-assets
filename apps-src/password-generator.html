About Passwords

Truly random. Every character and word comes from your iPhone's secure random generator (crypto.getRandomValues), picked without bias.

Private. Nothing is saved except your settings, and nothing leaves this page: no network, no AI, no history of what was made.

The strength numbers are exact for what this app makes: they count every secret these settings could produce. Crack times assume an offline attacker trying 10 billion guesses a second, and show the average time to find it.

PINs are short by nature. They are safe because the device locks after a few wrong tries, not because they are hard to guess.
