Welcome to
MyLLMos

The little OS that lives inside MyLLM — where apps you imagine become apps you own. Here's how it works.

swipe →

Step 1

Just ask

Describe any app in plain words. Watch MyLLM think, write real code, and hand it back as a tap-to-open card — right in the chat.

Step 2

Tap it — it's real

Your app opens full-screen and runs on your device — sliders, animation and all. No app store, no account, no waiting.

Step 3

Save it forever

Love it? Save to MyLLMos — give it a name, a colour and an icon. From that moment, it's yours.

Step 4

Your own springboard

Everything you save lives here — games, tools, trackers. Ask MyLLM to improve any of them and it edits the app for you.

Go further

Run your own LLM

MyLLM runs models right on this iPhone — or talks to your own computer. What to expect:

📱
This iPhoneNothing to set up. 1–3B models (Gemma, Qwen, Llama) run fully offline — chat, notes and quick tools.
💻
Your Mac or PCFree with Ollama + MyLLM Connect. 16 GB of RAM handles 7–8B models — a smooth daily driver.
🚀
A PC with a big GPU16 GB+ VRAM runs 14–27B models. Every screen in this tour ran on a 26B model on a home server.

Rule of thumb: bigger model → smarter apps. Your own cloud key works too.
