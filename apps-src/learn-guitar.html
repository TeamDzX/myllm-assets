How to hold it

Thumb behind the neck, roughly opposite your middle finger. Press just behind the fret, not on top of it. Curl your fingers so the tips land straight down — flat fingers mute the strings next door, which is the usual reason a chord buzzes.

    One-minute changes

    The real test of a beginner guitarist isn't knowing shapes, it's swapping between them. Pick two chords, start the clock, and tap every time you land the change cleanly. Both chords must ring — no muted strings.

    Strumming patterns

    Keep your hand moving down-up all the way through, even on the beats you don't hit. That steady motion is what keeps time — the missed strokes are just your hand passing the strings.
