About Pinpoint

Pinpoint turns where you are into a Plus Code, an open, free address for any spot on Earth, about 3 m across. It works offline; only the street name and town need a connection.

Plus Codes are the Open Location Code system by Google, published under the Apache 2.0 licence (open-location-code). QR codes are drawn by QR Code Generator for JavaScript, © Kazuhiko Arase, MIT licence. "QR Code" is a registered trademark of DENSO WAVE.

Street names come from your iPhone's own maps service.

Your location and saved places stay on this iPhone. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you share it.

The emergency card is a help, not a replacement for calling your local emergency number.
