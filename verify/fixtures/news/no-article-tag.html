An old-fashioned page with no semantic markup at all

Some sites still ship a page built entirely from unnamed divs, with nothing an extractor can key on except the shape of the prose itself.

That is the case this fixture covers: no article element, no itemprop, no role, just a well of text surrounded by navigation.

A density score handles it, because the well contains almost nothing but paragraphs while the page around it is mostly links.

If the scoring ever regresses to keying on tag names, this is the fixture that will notice before anybody's phone does.
